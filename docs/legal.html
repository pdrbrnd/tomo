Privacy & Terms

Short version: it's your library, on your Mac.

Privacy

Tomo is a local-first macOS app. Your library is a folder of files on your own disk — wherever you put it, including iCloud Drive if you choose. There is no Tomo server, no account, no login, no sign-up.

Crash reports. On by default, opt-out any time in Settings → Privacy. When something goes wrong, Tomo sends the crash stack trace and your macOS version to a hosted error tracker (Sentry) so I can fix it. Paths under your home folder are scrubbed before anything leaves your Mac. No library contents, no identifiers, no analytics, no usage tracking — ever.

Updates. Tomo checks tomolibrary.com/appcast.xml for new versions on launch (via Sparkle, the standard macOS updater). That's an ordinary web request with no personal data attached; you can turn automatic checks off.

Things you trigger. Cover and metadata lookups (Open Library, Apple, Google Books) and fetching the plugin registry happen only when you click. Search plugins you install make requests to their own third-party sources — those sites have their own terms, and what you install is your call.

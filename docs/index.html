Tomo

A native macOS app for the books on your shelves and the ones still to come.

  • Organise your library. Create collections, swap covers, and edit metadata.
  • Send to your kindle or kobo device. Just connect your device and drag your books into it.
  • Download new books. Use the included plugins to search common sources or create your own.
  • In-app reading. Open the files and read them without leaving the app. Preview books before adding to your library.
  • Privacy-first. No telemetry and nothing leaves your computer. The library is just a folder on your computer.
Download for macOS Get it from GitHub →

Or install via Homebrew:

brew install --cask pdrbrnd/tap/tomo

Requires macOS 26 or later.

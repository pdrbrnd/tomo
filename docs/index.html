Tomo

A native macOS library for the books on your shelves and the ones still to come.

  • A library, not a list. Group books into collections of your own, swap a cover when one doesn't fit, fix the details when they're off.
  • Send to e-reader. Plug it in, drag to device. Tomo handles the conversion and book covers, so they look right.
  • Find books without leaving the app. Search straight from the search bar. One click and they're in your library.
  • Knows your language. Brazilian Portuguese isn't European Portuguese. Your library tells them apart so they don't mix on the shelf.
  • Take it anywhere. Your library is a folder of files. Move it, sync it, copy it to another Mac. The books outlive the app.

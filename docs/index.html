Tomo

A native macOS app for the books on your shelves and the ones still to come.

  • Organise your library. Group books into collections of your own, swap a cover when one doesn't fit, fix the details when they're off.
  • Send to device. Plug your Kindle or Kobo in. Drag a book onto it. Format and cover sorted on the way over.
  • Search beyond your shelves. Add plugin sources from the official registry, or roll your own.
  • In-app reading. Preview a book's content before adding it to your library, be it from an external source or directly from Finder.
  • Take it anywhere. Your library is a folder of files. Move it, sync it, copy it to another Mac. The books outlive the app.
